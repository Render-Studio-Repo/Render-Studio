Browse and publish 3D models, knowledge bases, and agent apps. Set prices, connect Stripe or PayPal, and keep 90% of every sale.

Payments README

Render3D marketplace checkout uses Stripe (including Link one-click pay) so buyers can purchase agents, knowledge bases, and 3D models securely. Sellers connect a bank account through Stripe Express, or save a PayPal email for payout preference.

Platform split

Every paid listing takes a flat 10% platform fee. The remaining 90% goes to the seller.

How sellers get paid

  • Stripe / bank — Connect Express onboarding links your bank. Card & Link checkouts transfer net proceeds after the 10% fee.
  • PayPal — Save the PayPal email you want payouts associated with. Use it alongside Stripe while bank onboarding completes.
  • Free & view-only — No fee. Paid mode requires a connected payout method before you publish for sale.

Buyer checkout

  • Paid items open Stripe Checkout with card and Link.
  • After payment, agent installs unlock automatically; model/knowledge downloads follow the listing’s download toggle.
  • Minimum paid price is $1.00 (100 cents).
