                How it works
                
        
                
          A staged sculpting pipeline turns your reference into a spec, then generates and vision-reviews
          one build pass at a time —
          blockout → structural → form → material → surface → lighting → interaction → optimization
          — self-correcting until identity-defining features clear their thresholds.
          Deterministic scripts gate the work; model tokens are spent on visual judgment and code.
        
                
        
                
          Full pipeline diagram & token-efficiency design:
          docs/ARCHITECTURE.md
