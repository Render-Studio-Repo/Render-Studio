Hello World

A first note from Render Studio

This will probably be one of the few posts here that is hand written. I created this page for people who want to follow Render Studio more closely and see what we are building as the project evolves.

Render Studio is a browser-based 3D workspace for generating, editing, sharing, and collaborating on CAD models. The goal is to make mechanical design feel more direct: describe a part, revise it, inspect the component tree, simulate robot motion when URDF/SRDF data is available, and open shared models back into editable projects.

We are building toward opening Render Studio as an open-source project in August or September. Until then, this updates page will collect the bigger product changes, experiments, and workflow improvements so people can track where the platform is headed.
