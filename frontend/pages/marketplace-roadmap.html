Marketplace preview

Building the marketplace for robotics work

The marketplace is still in development. This is the direction we are building toward, not a promise that every capability is available today.

01

Creators and reusable work

Publish models, CAD generators, robot assets, skills, knowledge packs, electronics references, and complete project templates. Creator profiles, reviews, licensing, version history, and seller payouts will make reusable work easier to trust and maintain.

02

Verified robotics assets

Listings should expose supported robots, machines, formats, dependencies, licenses, safety assumptions, and validation evidence. Verified badges will reflect reproducible checks rather than marketing claims.

03

Paid cloud simulation compute

Teams will be able to purchase bounded cloud jobs for robot simulation, policy evaluation, synthetic data generation, and heavier CAD or mesh processing. Every job should show its estimated cost, limits, artifacts, logs, and completion state before more compute is charged.

04

Connected open-source instances

Independent Render installations can browse approved public listings through limited APIs. Identity, paid entitlements, platform fees, premium downloads, abuse controls, and seller payouts remain enforced by Render’s hosted control plane.

05

Safe purchasing and support

Planned commerce includes clear license terms, compatibility declarations, secure checkout, refund policies, seller support, update notices, reporting, and revocation for unsafe or misleading listings.

06

Community-shaped priorities

Builders can tell us which robot platforms, simulation workloads, file formats, integrations, and seller tools matter first. Suggestions are reviewed as product input, not treated as automatic commitments.
